Aim and Research Objectives

Primary Project Aim

"Develop a trilingual, multimodal and privacy-conscious AI-based wellness companion capable of estimating emotional states and providing guided non-clinical support."

Specific Research Objectives

01

Facial Emotion Recognition

Real-time visual expression classification across 7 universal affective states via CNN.

02

Speech Emotion Recognition

Acoustic emotion estimation extracted from 40-dimensional MFCC audio features.

03

Multimodal Fusion & Stress

Asynchronous late fusion with polar conflict detection and stress mapping.

04

Stage-Aware Guided Support

Constrained conversational therapy using a CBT state machine and deterministic fallback rules.

05

Trilingual Accessibility

Complete interface and speech interaction in English, Sinhala, and Tamil.

06

Privacy-Conscious Processing

Temporary in-memory media processing with client-side sensor consent controls.
