Future Work & Strategic Roadmap

PHASE 1 • DATA & TEMPORAL MODELS

Paired Datasets & Transformers

Train on synchronized audio-visual corpora (e.g. CREMA-D); deploy Vision Transformers (ViT) for FER and wav2vec 2.0 / LSTM for temporal speech dynamics.

PHASE 2 • ADVANCED FUSION

Attention-Based Dynamic Fusion

Replace static 50/50 probability averaging with learned cross-attention mechanisms that dynamically weight modalities based on acoustic SNR and face visibility.

PHASE 3 • PHYSIOLOGICAL SENSING

Wearable Biometric Telemetry

Integrate Bluetooth smart bands for non-invasive Heart Rate Variability (HRV) and Electrodermal Activity (EDA) to enhance objective stress profiling.

PHASE 4 • CLINICAL VALIDATION

Longitudinal Clinical Evaluation

Conduct an IRB-approved multi-user study in collaboration with certified psychiatrists to validate longitudinal therapeutic efficacy in Sri Lankan clinical settings.

Strategic Vision: Evolving MindCare from a non-clinical affective companion into an evidence-based digital therapeutic platform for low-resource global mental health.
