Critical Evaluation: System Limitations

01 • Visual Domain Gap (FER-2013)

ML Model

FER-2013 contains low-resolution (48×48) grayscale crops with ~65% human agreement ceiling. MobileNetV2 underperformed baseline CNN (40.53% vs 61.90%) due to ImageNet feature mismatch.

02 • Platform Dependency (TTS)

Host OS

Browser-native neural voice synthesis in Sinhala and Tamil relies on OS-level voice pack availability, requiring cloud fallbacks on unsupported client environments.

03 • Environmental Sensitivity

Acoustics & Light

Speech emotion recognition is susceptible to background ambient noise and microphone hardware; facial classification degrades under poor illumination and head rotation.

04 • Evaluation Constraint & Scope

Evaluation

No single unified multimodal benchmark metric exists because models were evaluated independently; system is strictly non-clinical without formal longitudinal trials.

Academic Integrity Note: Empirical limitations are presented transparently as engineering findings rather than failures; no unified multimodal accuracy is claimed without paired benchmark verification.
