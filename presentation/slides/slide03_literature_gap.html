Literature Review & Research Gap

01 • UNIMODAL SENSING

Environmental Sensitivity

FER is sensitive to lighting & posture (Dinges et al., 2005); SER degrades under ambient noise; text increases friction during panic (Suri et al., 2026).

02 • SENSOR FUSION

Multimodal Fusion

Combining visual and vocal channels enhances stability (Zhang et al., 2022); Late Fusion provides modularity and missing-sensor resilience.

03 • AI GOVERNANCE

Conversational AI Safety

Unrestricted LLMs carry hallucination and sycophancy risks in mental health; requires constrained graph workflows (Patel et al., 2026; Voultsiou & Moussiades, 2026).

04 • LOCALIZATION

Multilingual Accessibility

Over 90% of affective systems are English-centric (Zhu, 2023), causing performance degradation and exclusion in South Asian (Sinhala/Tamil) contexts.

The Identified Research Gap

A critical lack of accessible, low-barrier systems that integrate multimodal affective sensing, native trilingual support, and constrained conversational safety within a unified non-clinical wellness platform.
