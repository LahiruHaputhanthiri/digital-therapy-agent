Conclusion

Key Project Outcomes

  • Trilingual & Multimodal Companion: Developed an accessible, privacy-conscious AI-based wellness platform.
  • Affective Sensing: Integrated facial and speech emotion recognition to estimate emotional states.
  • Multimodal Late Fusion: Combined visual and acoustic probability distributions with conflict logic.
  • Constrained CBT Guidance: Implemented a 5-stage CBT state machine with deterministic local fallback.
  • Cultural Localization: Delivered English, Sinhala, and Tamil accessibility with speech interaction.
  • Privacy-Conscious Architecture: Enforced in-memory media processing and user consent controls.
  • Rigorous Verification: Validated core system functionality through 13 / 13 automated tests passed.

Key Finding

The empirical evaluation demonstrated the feasibility of combining multimodal emotion recognition with guided non-clinical wellness support, while also identifying limitations in individual model performance, environmental robustness and multimodal benchmark evaluation.

Final Takeaway

MindCare demonstrates a practical foundation for an accessible, multilingual and safety-conscious AI-assisted wellness companion.
